# EMG Signal Morphology Reference

This repository focuses entirely on EMG. A typical EMG signal is characterized by:

- bursts of muscle activity;
- variable amplitude over time;
- strong sensitivity to electrode placement and noise;
- a frequency content that reflects muscle physiology and contraction intensity.

## Why this matters in teaching

Understanding the natural morphology of EMG helps students appreciate why preprocessing and feature extraction are essential. Unlike a simple periodic signal, EMG often contains irregular bursts that require careful interpretation.
